Find the Wordle

New guesses start with five gray tiles. Tap once for yellow, twice for green, or a third time to return to gray. Results update as you tap. You can also enter all five colors like salet 02100 (0 gray, 1 yellow, 2 green).

Quick filter (letters without whole guesses)

How it works

Each candidate is scored against your guesses using Wordle's rules, including repeated letters. It stays on the list if every marked tile matches. Early suggestions favor guesses that split up the remaining words. With two turns left, they favor guesses that can leave a single answer. Classic answers carry more weight, and past answers stay eligible.
