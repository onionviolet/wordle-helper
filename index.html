How it works

Every past guess is rescored against each word exactly the way Wordle scores it, so repeated letters come out right. A word stays on the list only if it would have produced every color you saw. The best next guess is the word whose color pattern splits the remaining words into the most even groups, weighted so classic answers count more than rare words. NYT has reused past answers since February 2026, so earlier answers stay on the list.
